2.	Display the customer names and movie number of all customers who have been issued a movie.

Query:
SELECT C.FNAME,C.LNAME,I.MV_NO FROM CUSTOMER C,INVOICE I WHERE C.CUST_ID=I.CUST_ID;

3.	Display the movie number and title that has been issued to Ivan.

Query:
SELECT M.MV_NO,M.TITLE FROM MOVIE M,INVOICE I,CUSTOMER C WHERE M.MV_NO=I.MV_NO AND I.CUST_ID=C.CUST_ID AND C.FNAME='Ivan';

4.	Display title, customer id, movie number for all movies that are issued.

Query:
SELECT M.TITLE,I.CUST_ID,I.MV_NO FROM MOVIE M,INVOICE I WHERE M.MV_NO=I.MV_NO;

5.	Display the title and type of the movie that have been issued to Vandana.

Query:
SELECT M.TITLE, M.TYPE FROM MOVIE M, INVOICE I, CUSTOMER C WHERE M.MV_NO=I.MV_NO AND I.CUST_ID=C.CUST_ID AND C.FNAME='Vandana';


6.	Find the name of all customers who have been issued the movie of type action.

Query:
SELECT DISTINCT C.FNAME, C.LNAME FROM CUSTOMER C, INVOICE I, MOVIE M WHERE C.CUST_ID = I.CUST_ID AND I.MV_NO = M.MV_NO AND M.TYPE='Action';

7.	Display the movie title , last name, first name of all customers having the movie number greater than equal to 3 in the following format-
The movie taken by {Fname} {Lname} is {Title}.

Query:
SELECT 'The movie taken by ' || C.FNAME || ' ' || C.LNAME || ' is ' || M.TITLE || '.' AS DETAILS FROM CUSTOMER C, INVOICE I, MOVIE M
WHERE C.CUST_ID = I.CUST_ID AND I.MV_NO=M.MV_NO AND I.MV_NO>=3;

8.	Display the customer who has been issued the movie having invoice no ‘I10’.S

Query:
SELECT C.FNAME, C.LNAME FROM CUSTOMER C, INVOICE I WHERE C.CUST_ID=I.CUST_ID AND I.INV_NO='I10';

9.	Display the customer name, phone and area who have been issued the movie before the month of August.

Query:
SELECT C.FNAME, C.LNAME, C.PHONE,C.AREA FROM CUSTOMER C, INVOICE I WHERE C.CUST_ID=I.CUST_ID AND I.ISSUE_DATE<TO_DATE('01-AUG'-1993','DD-MON-YYYY');

10.	Display the movie number and title that has been issued to Ivan, Vandana and Ravi.

Query:
SELECT M.MV_NO,M.TITLE FROM MOVIE M,INVOICE I,CUSTOMER C WHERE M.MV_NO=I.MV_NO AND I.CUST_ID=C.CUST_ID AND C.FNAME IN('Ivan','Vandana','Ravi');

11.	List the movie number, movie name issued to customers.

Query:
SELECT M.MV_NO,M.TITLE FROM MOVIE M, INVOICE I WHERE M.MV_NO=I.MV_NO;

12.	Find the type and movie numbers of movies issued to customer id ‘A01’ and ‘A02’.

Query:
SELECT M.TYPE,M.MV_NO 
FROM MOVIE M,INVOICE I 
WHERE M.MV_NO=I.MV_NO 
AND I.CUST_ID IN('A01','A02');


13.	Find out if the movie whose star is Tom Cruise has been issued to any customer and print the customer_id to whom it has been issued.

Query:
SELECT I.CUST_ID FROM MOVIE M, INVOICE I WHERE M.MV_NO = I.MV_NO AND M.STAR='TC';

14.	Display the customer first name and last name who has been issued a movie.

Query:
SELECT DISTINCT C.FNAME, C.LNAME FROM CUSTOMER C,INVOICE I WHERE C.CUST_ID = I.CUST_ID;
